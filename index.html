A transparent, 3-step path from seat reservation to accredited academic portfolio credentials.

01

Register: Reserve Online

02

Attend: Fast Event Check-In

Admissions & Governance

Account Registration Approval Workflow

Non-members can submit an account request to access the chapter portal. To protect university standards and seat allocations, applications are manually cross-referenced with the collegiate student roster and activated by an executive officer within 24 hours.

1
Submit Academic Credentials Provide your official Student ID Number, Full Legal Name, Degree Program & Year, and University Email.
2
Officer Roster Verification (24-Hour SLA) An authorized executive officer verifies your active student standing and approves your digital chapter membership.
3
Instant Chapter Portal & QR Pass Activation Log in with your Student ID or Email to reserve event passes, access door check-in, and download certificates.
